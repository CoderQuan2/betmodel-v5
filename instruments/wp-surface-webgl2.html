WIN-PROBABILITY SURFACE

Height = win % for the team with the ball, over score difference and time left. The surface is the lab's model, computed live: a normal curve on the final margin, spread den = 13.5·√(t/3600) + 0.8 — the later it gets, the steeper the cliff at diff 0.

RAW WebGL 2.0 — no libraries. The mesh is a real indexed grid with computed normals, lit with Blinn-Phong in GLSL ES 3.00; journeys ride the vertex-shader ribbon pipeline from the volatility build. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
